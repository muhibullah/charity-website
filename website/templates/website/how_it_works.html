How it works

From your pocket to someone who needs it

No middlemen, no big overheads. Here's exactly what happens to your money.

  1. 1. You join

    Sign up on our Donate page and choose an amount - most people give £10 or £20 a month. You can also give once.

  2. 2. You give each month

    Pay by card or bank standing order. Every payment is recorded, so you always know what you've given.

  3. 3. Members suggest who to help

    Our members know people who are struggling - a family behind on rent, an elderly neighbour who can't afford heating, someone facing a medical bill. Each case is checked before we help.

  4. 4. We agree together

    Each month the group decides where the pooled money goes, based on who needs it most.

  5. 5. The help goes out

    We give cash, bank transfers or essentials such as food parcels and bill payments - directly, with nothing taken off.

  6. 6. We report back

    Every month's money in and money out is published on Where the money goes. With permission, we share stories too.

Privacy and dignity come first

The people we help never have their full names, addresses or photos published unless they choose to share their story. Donors are shown as "Anonymous" unless they ask to be named.
