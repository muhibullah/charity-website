{{ site_name }} is a community giving group run by volunteers. This page explains what personal information we keep and why.

What we collect

  • Donors: your name, email, phone number (optional), how much you give and when.
  • People we help: only what we need to help them. This is never published without their permission.

Why we use it

To record donations, contact you about your giving, and show accurate totals. We rely on your consent, which you give when you sign up.

Who can see it

Only the volunteers who run the group, through a password-protected admin area. We never sell or share your details. Card payments, when available, will be handled by Stripe, and we will not see your card number.

How long we keep it

While you are a member, and for up to 6 years after your last donation for our records. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your information, or withdraw your consent at any time. {% if site_settings.contact_email %}Email us at {{ site_settings.contact_email }}.{% else %}Contact us.{% endif %} If you're unhappy, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Cookies

We use only essential cookies needed for the website and forms to work. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
